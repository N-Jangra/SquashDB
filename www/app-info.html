App Info

What SquashDB is, and how it works.

What it does

SquashDB is a personal watchlist and progress tracker for TV series, K-dramas, C-dramas, anime, movies, manga, novels, and games. You track what you're watching, reading, or playing — status, ratings, notes, episode/volume progress — and browse online metadata sources to look up new titles without leaving the app.

How it works

Everything you track lives locally on your device — nothing is sent to a remote server. Data is stored in your browser's local storage and, if you've chosen a backup folder in Settings → Backups, mirrored as a plain folder tree (squash-db/<category>/<title>/index.json + a poster thumbnail) that you can back up, sync, or inspect yourself.

Discovering new titles is optional and online-only: SquashDB calls public metadata APIs (TVmaze, Wikidata, Open Library, AniList, and others you enable in Settings → Metadata Sources) directly from the app. A title only gets saved to your list once you open it and change something — status, rating, an episode tick, a note.

Offline-first

You can track everything by hand with Metadata Mode set to Offline — no internet connection needed. Switching it to Online adds the ability to search and auto-fill details, but tracking itself never requires a connection.
